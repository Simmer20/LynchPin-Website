ITM Africa is a professional services organization operating across Africa, providing solutions that support organizations in areas including talent, human capital, and workforce development.

View Full Case Study

Who is ITM Africa?

ITM Africa is a professional services organization operating across Africa, providing solutions that support organizations in areas including talent, human capital, and workforce development.

The Partnership

LynchPin partnered with ITM Africa to facilitate and moderate a panel discussion exploring the Future of Work, with a focus on Artificial Intelligence and Robotics.

The discussion brought together perspectives on how emerging technologies are reshaping the workplace and what organizations need to consider as they prepare for the future.

What LynchPin Did

Panel Facilitation & Moderation

Facilitating and moderating the discussion, guiding the conversation around three key questions:

  • AI & the Future of Work: How will artificial intelligence change the way organizations and people work?
  • Robotics in the Workplace: How will robotics be incorporated into workplaces across Africa and globally?
  • AI & Talent Strategy: How can organizations such as ITM incorporate AI into existing staffing, recruitment, and talent processes?

The Conversation

The session created a space for students, professionals, and industry stakeholders to explore how organizations can prepare for a rapidly changing workforce and how Africa can participate meaningfully in that transition.
